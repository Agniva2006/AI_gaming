📋 Tactical Playbook & Football Systems

Real-world tactical philosophies modifying defensive line height, compactness, pressing intensity, and pass selection.

1. Balanced Standard

Default tactical shape. Balanced lines, standard pressing intensity, and structured position holding.

2. Tiki-Taka Possession

High compactness, short rapid passing triangles, and high possession tempo to unlock stubborn low blocks.

3. Gegenpress High Press

Aggressive high defensive line with relentless pressing on the ball carrier to trigger immediate turnovers in the final third.

4. Counter-Attack Direct

Deep defensive low block drawing the opponent forward, followed by lightning vertical through-balls to sprinting attackers.

5. Park The Bus Defense

Ultra-compact penalty box wall minimizing space for through-balls, disciplined marking to defend narrow leads.
